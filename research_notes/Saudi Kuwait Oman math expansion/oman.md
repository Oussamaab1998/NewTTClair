# Oman: secondary mathematics, the General Education Diploma, and the market for a math exam-prep app (state as of November 2026)

Scope: Oman only. Researched 2026-11-17. Searches were run in English and Arabic. Where a figure is older than 2025/26, its year is given. "GED" below means the Omani General Education Diploma (دبلوم التعليم العام), not the US GED.

---

## 1. Structure of secondary math: grades, Core vs Advanced, the Cambridge series, semesters, reforms

### Takeaway
Omani schooling runs grades 1–10 (Basic Education) and grades 11–12 (Post-Basic). Since the 2022/23 study plan, every grade 11 student picks **Basic math (رياضيات أساسية)** or **Advanced math (رياضيات متقدمة)** and keeps that choice in grade 12. The two options replaced the old Applied and Pure math. All math from grades 1 to 12 now uses a Cambridge University Press series adapted for Oman. It reached grade 11 in 2022/23 and grade 12 in 2023/24. Advanced math is a Cambridge AS/A-Level Pure 1 + Probability & Statistics 1 adaptation: circular measure, trigonometry, limits and continuity, differentiation (including exponential functions), and probability with discrete random variables.

### Cited Findings
**System structure**
- The structure is Cycle One (grades 1–4, coeducational, 175 days/yr), Cycle Two (grades 5–10, separate boys' and girls' schools, 207 days/yr including exam days) and Post-basic/secondary (grades 11–12, separate boys' and girls' schools, 207 days/yr). Every level has 1,600 instructional minutes per week. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman (Ministry of Education authors)](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)
- The Ministry of Education manages grades 1–12. It sets policy, curricula, schoolbooks and assessment methods, and approves the curricula of all private schools. Monolingual private schools teach in Arabic. Bilingual private schools teach in Arabic and English. International schools follow their home-country programs (India, UK, US, France, etc.). The private sector "continues to play a relatively small role in Oman" compared with other Gulf countries. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)
- The year has two semesters. There is an exam at the end of semester 1 in January and another at the end of semester 2. Results from the two semesters are weighted equally in the year's overall average. Students in grades 5–12 get four report cards a year: two descriptive (mid-semester) and two with letter and numeric grades (end of semester). The pass mark is 50/100. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)

**Basic vs Advanced math**
- Under the 2022/23 study plan, grade 11 students choose one of two math subjects, basic or advanced. Applied Mathematics was merged into Basic Mathematics and Pure Mathematics into Advanced Mathematics. In grade 12, students must keep the science and math subjects they chose in grade 11. The three sciences (biology, physics, chemistry) became optional: students can take all three, two or one. About **101,583** grade 10 and grade 11 students in government and private schools made subject choices in March 2022. — [Oman Daily, 15 Mar 2022](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1017892)
- Who takes which: in the first-semester GED Advanced Math exam, **24,619** candidates sat it in 2023/24 (11,771 male, 12,848 female) and **28,659** in 2024/25 (13,621 male, 15,038 female). Candidates were regular students in government and private schools. The 2024/25 split by governorate was Muscat 6,499; North Batinah 4,918; Dakhiliya 4,462; South Batinah 3,446; South Sharqiya 2,384; Dhofar 2,118; North Sharqiya 2,092; Dhahira 1,612; Buraimi 707; Wusta 219; Musandam 202. The numbers come from Ministry data. — [Al-Hinai, MA thesis, A'Sharqiyah University 2025, Table 5](https://www.asu.edu.om/img/Dissertations/Dissertations_2025_m4d6_125438.pdf)

**The Cambridge-adapted series**
- The Ministry introduced the Cambridge mathematics curriculum gradually in all public schools: grades 1–4 in 2017–18, grades 5–6 in 2018–19, grades 7–8 in 2019–20, grade 9 in 2020–21, grade 10 in 2021–22, grade 11 in 2022–23 and grade 12 in 2023–24. Science followed the same schedule. TIMSS describes it as "a customized curriculum by Oman". — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)
- The Grade 11 Advanced Mathematics Student Book (2022 trial edition, semester 2) carries the copyright of Cambridge University Press and the Omani Ministry of Education. It states that it is adapted (تمت مواءمتها) from *Cambridge International AS & A Level Pure Mathematics 1* (Sue Pemberton), *Probability & Statistics 1* (Dean Chalmers) and *Further Mathematics* (Lee Mckelvey and Martin Crozier), under a contract between the Ministry and CUP. — [Grade 11 Advanced Math Student Book, Unit 9, 2022 trial edition (archive.org copy)](https://archive.org/download/11-2_20230408/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%AF%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A9%20%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9%20%20%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9%2011%20%D9%812%20%20%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8.pdf)

**Topics**
- Grade 11 Advanced, semester 2, Unit 9 is probability: counting, random variables and probability distribution tables (exercise set 9-2, p. 98). Its prerequisites page points back to grade 10 (units 10 and 12) and grade 9 (unit 9) for probability, sample-space and tree diagrams, and Venn diagrams. — [Grade 11 Advanced Student Book, Unit 9](https://archive.org/download/11-2_20230408/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%AF%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A9%20%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9%20%20%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9%2011%20%D9%812%20%20%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8.pdf)
- The Grade 12 Advanced semester-1 GED exam covers four units, with marks out of 70: Circular measure (القياس الدائري) 13, Trigonometry (حساب المثلثات) 22, Introduction to limits and continuity (مقدمة في النهايات والاتصال) 16, Differentiation (التفاضل) 19. — [Al-Hinai thesis 2025, Table 6](https://www.asu.edu.om/img/Dissertations/Dissertations_2025_m4d6_125438.pdf)
- A Grade 12 Advanced lesson plan for 2023/24 (Dhofar) puts "derivatives of exponential functions" in Unit 4, Differentiation, as lesson 4-3. Another plan has Unit 1, Circular measure, opening with radians. — [Teacher lesson plan, 12 Advanced, Unit 4](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ChVDrzZ9hZyEOWOseK_Paic77HGaHa9v/export?format=pdf); [Teacher lesson plan, 12 Advanced, Unit 1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Wqka8Lb002SDritqBjmFDn2XRW2RRLQl/export?format=pdf)
- Grade 12 **Basic** math (2023/24) has Unit 3, "Discrete random variables", with sample space and random-experiment concepts. — [Teacher lesson plan, 12 Basic, Unit 3 (North Batinah)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Se0TUwBmxkioowfIrOSjOgIpqYivBADy/export?format=pdf)
- The general math strands for grades 1–12 are Number and Number Theory; Number Operations; Geometry, Trigonometry and Spatial Sense; Measurement; Prealgebra and Algebra; Data Management and Probabilities; and Problem-Solving. Calculator use is built into the curriculum. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)

**Reforms (Vision 2040, 2023 law, tracks)**
- Royal Decree 31/2023 issued the School Education Law (قانون التعليم المدرسي). It is dated 27 Shawwal 1444 / 18 May 2023 and appeared in Official Gazette no. 1494. — [Law text (wisal.fm PDF)](https://static.wisal.fm/media/content_uploads/oldsite/uploads/2023/05/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A.pdf); [qanoon.om](https://qanoon.om/p/2023/rd2023031/); [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1137363)
- Ministerial Decision 225/2024 amends Decision 49/2020 on recognising qualifications of grade-12 completers. It cites the 2023 School Education Law. — [qanoon.om](https://qanoon.om/p/2024/moe20240225/)
- The vocational/technical track in grades 11–12 had 9 specialisations in 4 governorates and 1,253 students in 2025/26. In 2026/27 it expands to 6 governorates, adding South Sharqiya and South Batinah. Grade 12 adds Chinese as an elective, and grade 11 gets an updated IT curriculum. The 2026/27 start-of-year release announced **no math changes for grades 11–12**. Grade 4 got a new math curriculum. — [Oman Daily, 5 Sep 2026](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1209942)
- In Sept 2026 the Ministry announced it would expand specialised post-basic pathways in "a number of schools": engineering, industrial studies, travel and tourism, and IT, with business administration and IT continuing. The announcement did not mention math requirements or effects on the GED. — [Oman Observer, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1195904)
- Earlier Council of Ministers approval: grades 11 and 12 get vocational and technical education options, including engineering and industrial specialisations. — [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1133351)
- A new study plan for grades 1–4 started in 2024. It cut subjects from 11 to 10 and split them into basic and accompanying groups. — [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1158413)

### Inferences
- About half of each GED cohort takes Advanced math: 28,659 Advanced candidates against roughly 55k mainstream GED candidates in 2024/25. Advanced-math students are the natural first target, because their exam is calculus- and probability-heavy and feeds STEM and medicine admissions. Basic math is the other half and is likely the larger "struggling student" pool.
- Advanced math is a Cambridge AS-level Pure 1 / Stats 1 syllabus translated into Arabic. Its content overlaps heavily with Tunisian bac math (limits, derivatives, exponentials, probability, discrete random variables). Engine logic (`src/engine`) for derivatives, limits and probability could be reused. Exercise style follows Cambridge AO1/AO2 conventions, not French-style long problems.
- The Advanced cohort grew 16% in one year (24,619 → 28,659). This suggests more students are choosing Advanced, though cohort size may also have grown.

### Gaps
- No official full unit list was found for Grade 12 Advanced semester 2 (probably integration; maybe vectors or further statistics) or for Grade 11/12 Basic math. Teacher sites have the books, but I did not open them all.
- I found no official Ministry statistic on the Basic vs Advanced split for grade 12. Only the Advanced count from the thesis is available.
- I did not read the 2023 School Education Law's articles on the diploma, so any direct changes to the GED are unconfirmed.
- Some 2026 Arabic coverage calls the ministry "وزارة التعليم" instead of "وزارة التربية والتعليم". I did not verify whether it was renamed.

## 2. The exam that matters: the General Education Diploma (grade 12)

### Takeaway
The GED is a centrally set and centrally marked grade 12 exam, sat twice a year: semester 1 in late January and semester 2 at the end of the year. Each semester's exam is worth **70%** of the subject grade and continuous assessment 30%. The two semesters count equally. The Advanced math paper lasts **3 hours**, is worth 70 marks and has 27–29 compulsory items: 12 one-mark MCQs, short answers worth 2–3 marks and multi-step items worth 5 marks. About 55,000 mainstream candidates sit it each year (about 61,000 counting all categories). Final pass rates are about 83%. Math is regularly cited as the hardest paper.

### Cited Findings
**Administration and weighting**
- Grade 12 exams are administered and scored centrally. Grades 10–11 exams are set regionally and scored by schools. Weighting for grade 12 is **70% examinations / 30% continuous assessment**; for grades 10–11 it is 60/40, and for grades 5–9 40/60. The source is the Ministry's 2023 General Assessment Framework. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman, Exhibit 7](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)
- The Center for Educational Assessment and Measurement (مركز القياس والتقويم) writes the student-assessment documents for every subject and grade. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)

**Advanced math paper format (semester 1, 2023/24 and 2024/25)**
- The paper has two forms of 27 and 29 items, all compulsory, each measuring a single learning outcome. Time allowed is **3 hours**. Item types are 12 MCQs at 1 mark each (placed anywhere in the paper), short-answer free-response items at 2–3 marks, and long multi-step items at 5 marks. The total is **70 marks**. — [Al-Hinai thesis 2025, pp. 65–66](https://www.asu.edu.om/img/Dissertations/Dissertations_2025_m4d6_125438.pdf)
- The paper follows Cambridge assessment objectives. AO1, knowledge and understanding, is worth 50% (35 marks), and AO2, application and reasoning, is worth 50% (35 marks). Within each objective, difficulty splits 40% low, 40% medium and 20% high (14/14/7 marks). Papers are built from the Ministry's "student learning assessment document" for grades 11 and 12 and reviewed by a separate panel of judges. — [Al-Hinai thesis 2025, pp. 66–69, Tables 6–8](https://www.asu.edu.om/img/Dissertations/Dissertations_2025_m4d6_125438.pdf)

**Sessions and logistics**
- 2024/25 semester 1: **61,257** candidates from government, private, special-education and continuing-education schools sat the GED in **366** centres. Exams began Thursday 23 Jan 2025 with **Advanced and Basic Mathematics**. There were 4 marking centres running morning and evening shifts, and more than 6,200 teacher-proctors. (The article's "2025/2026" label conflicts with its Hijri date, 21 Rajab 1446, which places it in the 2024/25 year.) — [Oman Daily, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1171718)
- Results go out by SMS (seat number to 90200 on Omantel, Ooredoo or Vodafone), through the Parent App and through the Ministry's Educational Portal. Grade reviews (تظلمات) are requested through the portal for a fee. In 2026 the review window ran 17–23 July. — [Oman Observer, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1192903/oman/education/ged-results-to-be-out-from-today); [Oman Daily (Arabic), 2025](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1174475)

**Pass rates (most recent first)**
- **2025/26 final (results 15–16 Jul 2026):** mainstream GED **83.37%**. Private bilingual schools 92.39%; Royal Guard Technical College (bilingual) 94.55%; GED Islamic Studies 100%; special education 95.65%; vocational and technical 82.6%. The candidate count was not given. — [Oman Observer, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1192903/oman/education/ged-results-to-be-out-from-today)
- **2025/26 semester 1 (results 11–12 Feb 2026):** mainstream GED **81.15%**. Private bilingual 92.73%; Royal Guard Tech 100%; Islamic Studies 100%; special education 89.13%; vocational 81.5%. — [Oman Observer, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1184247). Conflicting report: [Muscat Daily](https://www.muscatdaily.com/?p=97920) pairs 81.15% with 54,856 candidates. That count matches the July 2025 Ministry release, so Muscat Daily may have mixed up figures.
- Students reacting to the 2025/26 semester-1 results blamed lower marks on **difficult mathematics and physics exams**. — [Oman Daily (Feb 2026)](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1196773)
- **2024/25 final (results 17 Jul 2025):** **54,856** candidates, overall **82.83%**. Private bilingual 91.58%; Royal Guard Tech 100%; Islamic Sciences 100%; special education 86.89%; vocational 100%. — [Oman Observer, 16 Jul 2025](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1173565/oman/education/education-ministry-announces-results-of-general-education-diploma-exams); same figures from [Oman News Agency](https://omannews.gov.om/topics/en/79/show/123345/light)
- **2024/25 semester 1 (reported ~Mar 2025):** **54,943** GED candidates, **80.76%**. Bilingual private schools had 1,577 candidates at 91.05%; vocational and technical 138 candidates at 99.27%. — [Oman Daily (Arabic)](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1174475); [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1167274). Conflicting: [Al-Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5176606) reported 81.3% overall.

**Past papers**
- I found no official Ministry archive of GED past papers and model answers. Teacher-made quizzes, books and lesson plans circulate on community sites such as "مدونة سلطنة عمان التعليمية" (oman-edu.com, watermarked on the files I opened), on archive.org and in shared Google Docs. — [example quiz, archive.org](https://archive.org/download/3_20230503_202305/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%20%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%81%20%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%20%20%D8%B9%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85%203.pdf)

### Inferences
- Each GED math paper covers **one semester of content**: semester-1 Advanced is circular measure, trigonometry, limits and differentiation. Content packs can therefore be split by semester, and there are two "exam seasons" a year (January and May/June). That gives two marketing peaks rather than the single June peak of the Tunisian bac.
- The paper is short-item and Cambridge-style: 12 MCQs plus many 2–5 mark items. That suits an app built on small steps, options and "I don't know" hints better than long French-style problem statements. Our "exercise per official past paper" authoring model would have to work with unofficial copies, since I found no official archive.
- The pass rate (about 83%) is not the pain point. Competitive averages for university admission are (see section 3), so the product pitch should be "raise your score", not "pass".

### Gaps
- Second-round (الدور الثاني) / resit rules and dates for grade 12 were not confirmed. No source described a grade-12 second-round sitting.
- No exact dates or format were found for the semester-2 exam, and no official specification for the Basic math paper (duration, marks).
- Per-subject score statistics (math mean, distribution) are not published. The thesis has descriptive statistics (Table 10) that I did not extract.
- I did not find the 2025/26 candidate count.

## 3. University admission: HEAC, the competitive average, and math requirements

### Takeaway
All public places and most government-funded places go through the Higher Education Admission Center (HEAC, مركز القبول الموحد). It ranks students by a programme-specific competitive average: **60% the average of the programme's required subjects + 40% the average of all GED exam subjects**. Medicine cut-offs reached 98–99% in 2026. Math marks therefore count twice for STEM and medicine applicants. I could not confirm the current list of which math (Advanced or Basic) each programme requires.

### Cited Findings
- HEAC was established by Royal Decree 104/2005. Its formula: competitive score = (average grade of the subjects required for the programme) × 60% + (average grade of the exam subjects taken by the student) × 40%. — [HEAC, About us](https://heac.gov.om/index.php/en/about-us). An older HEAC guide (hosted by the Indian Embassy) states the same 60/40 split using sums. — [HEAC guide copy](https://www.indemb-oman.gov.in/docs/1629880293StudentGuideofOmaniProgramEn.pdf)
- Oman Observer describes the formula as weighing "60 per cent of relevant subject marks and 40 per cent of the general average". — [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/ampArticle/1173802)
- Required subjects vary by programme. The older (pre-2022) HEAC guide listed mathematics for engineering, **Pure Mathematics** for some programmes, and "Pure or Applied Mathematics" for business and economics, plus minimums such as a 75% average with at least 65% in listed subjects. — [HEAC guide copy (c. 2021)](https://www.indemb-oman.gov.in/docs/1629880293StudentGuideofOmaniProgramEn.pdf). For context, Muscat University's 2020 foundation entry required 65% overall, 65% English and 65% "Pure Maths OR Applied Maths". — [Muscat University, Aug 2020](https://muscatuniversity.edu.om/new-entry-requirements-for-undergraduate-programmes/)
- **2026/27 first round (announced 19 Aug 2026):** 46,934 eligible applicants; **33,031 offers** (70.4%) against 34,113 seats, of which 1,082 went unfilled. Offers split 17,430 government institutions, 14,950 internal scholarships at private institutions and 651 external scholarships. Cut-offs: SQU General Medicine **98.15% (male) / 99.2% (female)**; National University of Science and Technology medicine 98.3%; Dhofar University medicine 97.9%; Dentistry 95.1% / 97.55%; SQU Education 91.65% / 96.05%; external STEM scholarship 97.7%; ESAM 96.45%. — [Oman Observer, 19 Aug 2026](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1194753/oman/education/heac-extends-over-33000-academic-offers-in-first-admission-round)
- 2025/26 first round: 27,537 students accepted, with 95% of seats filled. — [Oman Daily](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1184721)
- HEAC publishes a yearly "guideline" of recommended minimum competitive averages per programme. — [HEAC guideline 2024/25](https://heac.gov.om/media/doc/GuideLines2024-2025.pdf). The heac.gov.om PDFs now return an HTML shell (the site moved to a single-page app), so their contents could not be read.

### Inferences
- Advanced Mathematics replaced Pure Mathematics, which older guides required for engineering and some science programmes. So Advanced math is very likely the required math for SQU engineering, science and medicine today. This is an inference from the 2022 renaming and the old Pure-math rules, not a confirmed current rule.
- With medicine cut-offs at 98–99%, top students need near-perfect math marks. A premium "score-maximiser" mode (exam-style drills, timing, AO2 reasoning items) would match real demand.

### Gaps
- I could not access the current HEAC programme-by-programme subject requirements (Advanced vs Basic math, minimum marks) or the SQU College of Engineering and Medicine subject lists.
- HEAC's subject-requirement tables need checking directly in the HEAC app or site, or the 2026/27 student guide.

## 4. Numbers: students, schools, GED candidates, TIMSS

### Takeaway
Government schools hold about **876k students in 1,298 schools (2026/27)**. Each grade-12 cohort is about **55k** mainstream GED candidates (about 61k across all categories). About **29k** of them take Advanced math, as of 2024/25. Oman's TIMSS 2023 math scores are well below the international average: grade 4 scored **421** (down from 431 in 2019) and grade 8 **411** (flat). Oman sits in the middle of the Gulf pack, above Kuwait and Saudi Arabia and below the UAE, Qatar and Bahrain.

### Cited Findings
**Enrolment and schools**
- **2026/27:** **876,117** students (440,899 male / 435,218 female) in **1,298** schools: 341 for grades 1–4, 128 for grades 5–8, 165 for grades 9–12 and 664 "continuing" schools. Government-school staff total 68,205, including 21,011 male and 47,194 female teachers. Private schools are expected to grow from 1,264 to 1,366, which is 24.6% growth over five years. — [Oman Daily, 5 Sep 2026](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1209942)
- **2025/26:** 66,379 government teachers in **1,303** government schools. — [Oman Daily, 24 Aug 2025](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1184983). Semester 2 of 2025/26 had about 843,235 government-school students. — [Oman Daily (Jan 2026), via search excerpt](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1195213)
- The Minister told the Shura Council that schools would number 1,306 in 2025/26, against 1,182 in 2020/21. — [Oman Daily](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1174009)
- Grade 10 and 11 cohorts together were about 101,583 students in March 2022 (government + private). — [Oman Daily, 15 Mar 2022](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1017892)

**GED candidates**
- 54,943 (semester 1, 2024/25) and 54,856 (final, 2024/25) mainstream GED candidates. 61,257 sat semester-1 exams across all categories in Jan 2025. Bilingual private schools had 1,577 candidates (2024/25 semester 1). — [Oman Daily](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1174475); [Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1173565/oman/education/education-ministry-announces-results-of-general-education-diploma-exams); [Oman Daily, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1171718)
- Advanced math GED candidates: 24,619 (2023/24) and 28,659 (2024/25). — [Al-Hinai thesis 2025](https://www.asu.edu.om/img/Dissertations/Dissertations_2025_m4d6_125438.pdf)

**TIMSS math**
- **TIMSS 2023 grade 8 math:** Oman **411** (SE 2.7); international average 478. Regional comparison: UAE 489, Qatar 451, Bahrain 426, Oman 411, Kuwait 399, Saudi Arabia 397, Jordan 388, Morocco 378. — [IEA TIMSS 2023, Exhibit 1.2.1](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/1-2-1_ach-g8m-dist.pdf)
- **TIMSS 2023 grade 4 math:** Oman **421** (SE 4.0); international average 503. Regional comparison: UAE 498, Qatar 464, Bahrain 462, Jordan 427, Oman 421, Saudi Arabia 420, Kuwait 382. — [IEA TIMSS 2023, Exhibit 1.1.1](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/1-1-1_ach-g4m-dist.pdf)
- **TIMSS 2019:** grade 4 math, Oman 431 (SE 3.7). — [IEA TIMSS 2019, grade 4 math chapter](https://timssandpirls.bc.edu/timss2019/international-results/wp-content/themes/timssandpirls/download-center/achievement/T19_Ch1-achievement-mathematics-4.pdf). Grade 8 math, Oman 411. — [Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/timss-assessment-a-global-indicator-of-education-level) (secondary source; its regional figures match the TIMSS 2019 grade 8 table).
- Oman took part in TIMSS 2007, 2011, 2015, 2019 and 2023. The Ministry attributes the low 2007 and 2011 scores to students not being used to TIMSS-style items. It responded with teacher training on cognitive-domain item writing, including a new programme in 2023–24. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)

### Inferences
- Addressable market: about 55–61k grade-12 students a year plus a similar grade-11 cohort, so about 110–120k post-basic students. Advanced-math students number about 29k per grade. This is roughly a fifth of Saudi scale and comparable to Tunisia's bac cohort.
- Weak and stagnant TIMSS scores (grade 4 fell 10 points from 2019 to 2023) support a "struggling student" positioning in Oman, consistent with our Tunisian design.

### Gaps
- I found no official NCSI breakdown of private-school student numbers or of grade 11/12 enrolment by grade. NCSI publications exist ([education statistics PDF](https://api.ncsi.gov.om/uploads/pdfs/education_statitics_1788432288.pdf)) but were not opened.
- A 2025/26 total of 854,540 students (430,461 boys / 424,079 girls) appeared in a search excerpt attributed to Oman Daily. I could not tie it to a specific article, so treat it as unverified.
- TIMSS 2023 benchmark percentages for Oman were not extracted.

## 5. Language and notation (critical for the renderer)

### Takeaway
Omani public-school math is written in **fully Arabic, right-to-left notation**, in both the official Cambridge-adapted textbooks and teacher material:
- Arabic-letter variables: س for x, ص for y, د(س) for f(x), هـ for e, ل(س) for P(X).
- **Eastern Arabic-Indic digits** (٠١٢٣…٩), with a decimal comma.
- Inequalities and radicals mirrored for right-to-left reading.
- Derivatives written ءص/ءس.

This is the opposite of Tunisia's French-style left-to-right LaTeX notation. Our KaTeX left-to-right assumption breaks for Oman's public schools (about 97% of GED candidates). Only bilingual private schools (about 1,600 GED candidates) teach math in English with Latin notation.

### Cited Findings
- **Official Grade 11 Advanced textbook (MoE + Cambridge University Press, 2022 trial edition, semester 2, p. 98, exercise 9-2).** Random variables are named with Arabic letters: "المتغير العشوائي (س)", "(ح)", "(ق)". Probability is written "ل(س)" for P(X). Unknown constants are "ب", "ك", "ج". Table values use Arabic-Indic digits (٢، ٣، ٤، ٥ / ٣، ٦، ٩، ١٢، ١٥), and fractions are written in Arabic-Indic digits (٤/٨١، ١/٨، ٧/٩). An interval appears as "ل(٢ > س > ٥)", the right-to-left form of P(2 < X < 5). Set notation appears as "ق ∈ {٢، ٣، ٤، ٥، ٦}". — [Grade 11 Advanced Student Book, Unit 9 (archive.org)](https://archive.org/download/11-2_20230408/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%AF%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A9%20%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9%20%20%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9%2011%20%D9%812%20%20%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8.pdf) (I rendered and viewed the pages)
- The same book uses Arabic-Indic digits throughout, for example "تمارين ٩-٢". Its prerequisite page uses set symbols (∪, complement ʹ) mixed with Arabic letters. — same source
- **Grade 12 Advanced** "result" boxes (نتيجة ٣، نتيجة ٤), reproduced from the student book in a 2023/24 lesson plan:
  - "ءَ/ءس (هـ^س) = هـ^س" (that is, d/dx(eˣ) = eˣ)
  - "إذا كانت ص = هـ^د(س) فإن ءَ/ءس (هـ^د(س)) = دَ(س) × هـ^د(س)" (if y = e^f(x), then dy/dx = f′(x)e^f(x))
  - "ءَ/ءس (هـ^(أس+ب)) = أهـ^(أس+ب)"
  - Exercises such as "ص = ٢هـ^٦س" and "ص = ٥√هـ^٣س − ١/هـ^٢س", with mirrored radicals and Arabic-letter exponents.

  — [Teacher lesson plan, 12 Advanced, Unit 4-3, Dhofar 2023/24](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ChVDrzZ9hZyEOWOseK_Paic77HGaHa9v/export?format=pdf) (I rendered and viewed the page)
- A teacher-made Grade 11 Advanced quiz (2023, oman-edu.com) follows the same conventions: random variable "س", "ل(س)", parameters "م، ك", Arabic-Indic digits and decimal comma "٠,٢٨", and a probability table laid out right to left. — [Quiz PDF (archive.org)](https://archive.org/download/3_20230503_202305/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%20%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%81%20%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%20%20%D8%B9%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85%203.pdf)
- Language of instruction: monolingual schools, including all government schools, teach in Arabic. Bilingual private schools teach in Arabic and English. International schools use their own languages and curricula. — [TIMSS 2023 Encyclopedia: Oman](https://timss2023.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Oman.pdf)
- Bilingual GED track: a school-directory site says that in the Omani bilingual GED, all subjects are taught in English except Arabic, Islamic Studies and Social Studies, so math and science are in English. Most bilingual schools pair the GED with IGCSE, A-Level or IB. — [Oman School Finder (secondary, commercial)](https://omanschoolfinder.com/schools/curriculum/bilingual-omani)
- Bilingual private schools had 1,577 GED candidates in 2024/25 semester 1, against 54,943 mainstream. — [Oman Daily](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1174475)

### Inferences
- **Renderer impact (high):** to serve Omani public-school students the way their textbook and exam look, we would need right-to-left Arabic math:
  - Arabic-letter symbols and Arabic-Indic digits
  - mirrored operators: inequalities, radical sign, possibly arrows and parentheses
  - the Arabic differential "ء"
  - Arabic exponent placement

  KaTeX has no built-in right-to-left / Arabic-math mode (this needs verifying against current KaTeX releases). Options are a custom Arabic-math layer (glyph mirroring with a transform, an Arabic math font such as one supporting the OpenType `rtlm` feature), server-side SVG rendering, or a different engine. This is a sizeable spike, and it directly conflicts with the CLAUDE.md rule that "Math is ALWAYS rendered as its own block, left-to-right".
- Using left-to-right Latin notation (x, f(x), digits 0–9) for Omani students would conflict with what they see in the textbook and on the GED paper. That carries a product risk of confusion and lower trust. It would still be fine for the bilingual or international niche (about 3% of GED candidates).
- Student input (typing answers) must accept Arabic-Indic digits and Arabic letters, and normalise them to the engine's internal form. The safe parser would need an Arabic-symbol front end.
- Explanations would be in Omani Arabic dialect (or Gulf MSA-light), not Tunisian derja. Arabic math terms are standard (نهاية، اتصال، تفاضل، مشتقة، متغير عشوائي، توزيع احتمالي), and French terms do not apply.

### Gaps
- I did not see an actual GED exam paper (only textbooks, teacher plans and quizzes). I infer that the GED paper uses the same notation, but it is not verified.
- I did not confirm limit notation (likely "نها") or trigonometric function names (likely جا، جتا، ظا) in the Omani grade 12 book.
- I did not confirm whether the bilingual GED math exam is a separate English-language paper set by the Ministry.

## 6. Study habits: private tutoring, Ministry platforms, apps

### Takeaway
Paid private tutoring is widespread before exams. Parents described it as having become "mandatory". Reported prices were **above 20 OMR/hour and about 1,200 OMR/year per subject** in 2019. Online international tutors charge about 20–40 OMR/hour. The Ministry is rolling out its own LMS ("Noor", with Classera) to all government schools from 2025/26. Local video-lesson services for diploma students have existed since 2015 (Dorous Oman).

### Cited Findings
- Parents complained that tutoring prices rise before semester exams, with the hourly rate exceeding **20 OMR** and about **1,200 OMR per year for one subject**. Pre-exam tutoring "has become mandatory for parents", mostly for science subjects. Reasons given were short class time, classes of about 30, crowded curricula and abrupt transitions between grades (Cambridge-based). — [Oman Daily, 17 Dec 2019](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/29644)
- "Dorous Oman" (دروس عمان) is a website founded in 2015 that offers video explanations of all subjects for diploma students. In 2018, Ooredoo sponsored a free app version, "Dorous Oman Digital", with video and audio lessons. — [Oman Daily, 17 Dec 2019](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/29644)
- Commercial tutoring benchmarks (international curricula):
  - IGCSE tutoring in Oman typically costs 20–40 OMR/hour, and TutorChase starts at 24 OMR/hour. — [TutorChase](https://www.tutorchase.com/igcse/oman-1)
  - Local marketplace tutors charge about 5–19 OMR/hour (Filo, Mutrah listing). — [Filo](https://askfilo.com/language-and-culture-online-tutors-mutrah)
  - These are commercial, self-reported figures.
- **Noor platform (منصة نور):** the Ministry's e-learning system and digital repository. Phase 1 launched in semester 1 of 2024/25 in four governorates, with rollout to all government schools in 2025/26. It provides digital content, course, assignment and activity management, virtual classes, parent access and performance tracking. The partner is Classera, and a project committee was set up by Ministerial Decision 84/2024. Oman won a WSIS (World Summit on the Information Society) prize for Noor. — [Oman Daily (WSIS prize)](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1206364); [Oman Daily (teacher training)](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1175350)
- The Ministry's Educational Portal (البوابة التعليمية) and Parent App (تطبيق ولي الأمر) are the official channels for results and grade reviews. — [Oman Observer, Jul 2026](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1192903/oman/education/ged-results-to-be-out-from-today); [Al-Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5006224)
- An Oman Daily piece describes a "Mawrid channel" (قناة مورد) as among the most enriching educational resources. I could not establish whether it is an official Ministry channel. — [Oman Daily](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1158354)
- Private firms reward top diploma students, for example "Oman Academy" (عُمان أكاديمي). This suggests a local edtech/tutoring market built around the GED. — [Oman Daily](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/1184174)
- The Ministry also runs in-school remedial classes (دروس التقوية) before tests. — [Oman Daily, 2019](https://www.omandaily.om/ampArticle/29644)

### Inferences
- At about 20 OMR/hour (about USD 52) for human tutoring, a subscription of a few OMR a month is a very cheap alternative. Willingness to pay appears to exist, especially in Muscat and among Advanced-math and STEM-bound students.
- Noor (Classera) is an LMS for teachers, schools and parents, not a step-by-step AI tutor. It is more likely a possible channel or partner (content integration) than a direct competitor. I found no evidence of a dominant Omani AI math tutoring app.
- Exam seasons (late January, May/June) and the July results window are the moments for acquisition.

### Gaps
- I found no recent (2023–2026) survey with tutoring prevalence percentages or current GED-math tutoring prices in OMR. An academic study by Rashid Al-Hajri on private tutoring in Oman exists but was not found in full text.
- I did not verify whether the Ministry has an official YouTube or TV channel with GED math revision lessons.
- App-store data (which tutoring or exam-prep apps Omani students use: Noon Academy, Abwaab, local apps) was not researched in this pass.
